## Complementary suppression and public output

Primary small outcome counts are nonzero values **1–4**, minimum cell size five. Hide the entire sensitive cell, including its derived shares, ratios, confidence intervals and verdicts. Additionally, **any small count anywhere in the linked statistical table suppresses all linked blocks together**: micro, all rule cells, repository-clustered results, exclusions and attempted/completed/failed/unattempted counts. This intentionally conservative whole-table suppression prevents subtracting visible siblings from a total or inverting precision/recall to reconstruct a hidden value. Do not publish independent numeric marginals or unsuppressed ratios/intervals that bypass this policy. **P4 / D1 option 2 deliberately publishes categorical micro gate verdicts outside these blocks**, under `micro_gates = {precision_gate, recall_gate}`, alongside fixed attempt status. This is the sole categorical exception: no per-rule verdicts or hidden counts are exposed. Attribution's implied counts `correct = round(attribution_accuracy × attribution_labelled)` and `incorrect = labelled − correct` are primary cells too; if either is 1–4, hide the entire linked numeric table. The B1 regression tests cover **2 wrong out of 60 and 1 wrong out of 20**. Other derived ratios already bind the explicit TP/FP/FN or unlabelled counts. Zero/empty cells stay descriptive or suppressed under the existing empty-cell rule; four-repository metadata is a predeclared shape, not a primary rare outcome.

Only allowlisted aggregate keys/enumerated values and provenance hashes may leave the private directory. Case IDs, repository identities, symbols, paths, diagnostic/exception prose and individual rows remain private. The fixed `complete`/`interrupted` attempt status and release/hash provenance can still be public when its whole statistical table is suppressed; the full quantitative report is retained for the independent audit. A suppressed numeric table can therefore still publish **“provisional pass” / “fail” / “insufficient data” for precision and “pass” / “fail” / “insufficient data” for recall**. The categories intentionally reveal **ranges**, not exact small counts: conditional on exactly 60 supported positive oracle root causes (the expected 60-positive-case shape alone does not prove one root per case), precision provisional-pass implies `FP ≤ 1` (Wilson requires at least 52 TP when FP=1), and recall pass implies `FN ≤ 12`; recall fail with sufficient denominators implies `FN ≥ 13`. Combined pass constrains these ranges jointly. Precision fail is the complement of the precision threshold within sufficient cells and **does not imply FP≥2** (even FP=0 can fail Wilson at low TP). Insufficient data identifies an inadequate denominator or interruption, not an exact count. A complete status reveals only completion; interrupted forces both micro categories to insufficient. These categorical range disclosures are explicitly authorized by P4 and are not hidden by complementary suppression. Numeric counts, ratios and intervals remain suppressed whenever a linked outcome is 1–4. The sealed evaluator skips ineligible cases, and the original seal places rejected scenarios outside `cases`; their emitted noise is **not measured**. Zero exclusions/unlabelled-case share here is largely by construction, not evidence of low noise on excluded cases. Only out-of-scope findings inside evaluated eligible cases contribute the measured unlabelled-prediction share. Public shape expectations above are not reverse-engineered from a measurement. Do not cross-publish differently suppressed views of the same table.
